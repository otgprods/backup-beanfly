Frequently asked questions

Is Beanfly free?

Yes. The core Beanfly shopping list is free to download and use. An optional subscription, Beanfly Plus, adds premium themes, mascot animations and the price tracker.

Does Beanfly have ads or tracking?

No. Beanfly has no ads, no tracking and no account. Your shopping lists stay on your device and nothing is sold or shared.

How does the aisle sorting work?

Type an item and Beanfly automatically files it into the right supermarket aisle, so milk, apples and bread fall into Dairy, Fruit & Veg and Bakery without any effort. You can also switch to your own custom order.

Does Beanfly work offline?

Yes. Beanfly works fully offline and never needs an internet connection.

What languages does Beanfly support?

Beanfly is available in 19 languages, with right-to-left support for Hebrew, Arabic and Urdu.

What is Beanfly Plus?

Beanfly Plus is an optional subscription that supports the private, ad-free app and unlocks premium themes, mascot animations and the price tracker, which shows how your prices change over time and lets you share a branded price card.
